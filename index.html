Horizons of Quantum Information III

Spacetime states meet quantum processes

1–5 March 2027

Nanyang Executive Centre Nanyang Technological University, Singapore

About

Horizons of Quantum Information is a workshop series on quantum correlations in time.

The third edition opens the series to the process-based view of multi-time quantum physics: process tensors, process matrices and quantum (non-)Markovianity. The state-based and process-based approaches both describe a quantum system probed at several times, but they start from different objects. One extends the notion of a state across time; the other characterises the process that connects interventions made at different times. Each has developed its own formalism and vocabulary.

Many of this year’s participants work on process tensors and non-Markovian dynamics. Over a week of invited talks and discussion, the aim is to set the two frameworks side by side, clarify how they relate, and identify problems where each can inform the other.

Topics

  • Pseudo-density operators, quantum states over time and spacetime states
  • Process tensors, process matrices and multi-time quantum processes
  • Quantum Markovianity, non-Markovianity and memory effects
  • Temporal and spatiotemporal quantum correlations
  • Relations between state-based and process-based frameworks

Accommodation

Participants can stay at the venue itself. NEC has 150 guest rooms, from Deluxe rooms for one or two guests to larger suites. As a guide, 2026 rates were S$190 per night for a Deluxe room and S$250 for an Executive Suite, including breakfast, plus 10% service charge and 9% GST. Rooms are reserved for guests of NTU, and booking details for participants will be shared closer to the workshop.

Rooms and facilities at NEC

Getting there from Changi Airport

By taxi or ride-hailing app, the trip takes about 40 minutes and costs around S$30–45, with surcharges at peak hours and after midnight. Give the address as 60 Nanyang View, Nanyang Technological University.

By MRT, ride from Changi Airport station (CG2) to Tanah Merah (EW4) and change to a westbound East–West Line train towards Tuas Link. Get off at Pioneer (EW28), then take bus 179 from Exit A or NTU’s free Campus Rider shuttle from Exit B into the campus. Allow about an hour and a half in total.
